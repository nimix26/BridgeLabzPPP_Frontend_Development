Shadows and Depth Effect

Card One

This card has a subtle box-shadow. On hover, it lifts up and the shadow becomes larger, creating a depth effect.

Card Two

This card has a deeper, more pronounced box-shadow by default. The hover effect is the same, enhancing the 3D feel.

Card Three

This card uses an inset shadow for a pressed-in look. On hover, it reverts to a standard "lifted" shadow.
